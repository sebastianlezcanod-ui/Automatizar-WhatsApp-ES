# Brag Plan: WA Direct Sender

## What is this app?
WA Direct Sender is an open-source, 100% private Manifest V3 Chrome Extension that turns WhatsApp Web into an unlimited bulk-messaging and automation power tool with Excel/CSV mapping, dynamic `{Variables}`, clean media attachments (zero "Forwarded" tag), and an intelligent anti-ban humanized pacing engine.

## The angle
"Tired of WhatsApp extensions charging $30/month just to cap you at 200 messages?"
Every existing Chrome extension puts up artificial paywalls, caps users, or routes private phone numbers through unknown external servers. WA Direct Sender gives power back to users with zero limits, client-side privacy, and professional features for free.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold callout: **"Paying $30/month for 200 WhatsApp messages? 🛑"**
Split-screen visual with high contrast: an obnoxious red "$29.99/mo subscription barrier" being shattered by a sleek WhatsApp green terminal beam.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1: Instant Excel & CSV Upload with Smart Preview (3–7s)**
  Drag-and-drop `.xlsx` sheet into the on-page drawer. Auto-detects phone columns and shows real-time monospace preview badges (`[919876543210]`, `[918765432109]`, `[917654321098]`) + dynamic variable insertion `{Name}`.
- **Moment 2: Dual Media Sharing with Zero "Forwarded" Tag (7–12s)**
  Send images, PDFs, or documents seamlessly without looking like cheap spam—no "Forwarded" badge.
- **Moment 3: Anti-Ban Protection & Top Safety Warning Banner (12–16s)**
  Live countdown with randomized human delays (5–15s). A prominent floating top banner alerts you not to disrupt the tab, with 1-click minimize/restore.

## Outro / punchline (16–20s)
"Zero Limits. No Paywalls. 100% Local & Private."
Official GitHub release call-to-action: `github.com/nil-frontend/WA-Automation` with animated star ⭐ icon and free ZIP download badge.
